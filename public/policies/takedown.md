# Module takedown policy

## Report a concern

If you believe a module on mooncakes.io infringes your intellectual property rights, please email [support@moonbitlang.com](mailto:support@moonbitlang.com?subject=Intellectual%20property%20complaint) with:

- Your name and contact information.
- The module name, affected versions, and links to the content.
- Evidence of ownership of the rights claimed, and authorization if you represent the rights holder.
- A description of the alleged infringement and supporting evidence.

We review complaints based on the materials provided and may request further information. We may remove a module or restrict access to affected content following review or as required by law.

## Request a review

If you believe your module was removed in error, email us with the module name and supporting information so we can review the decision.

## Publisher requests

To avoid breaking existing dependencies, we generally do not remove modules at the publisher's request, except in the circumstances described above. Please use `moon deprecate` to mark a module as deprecated instead.
